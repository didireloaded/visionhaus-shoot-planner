# Antislop audit 001 · 25 November 2026

Scope: the live booking app (Home, package detail, Rentals, My Plan builder, Saved, Studio, brief upload) at 390px and 1280px, plus `public/style.css` and the markup in `public/app.js`.
Method: rendered every screen in Chromium, ran an automated pass for contrast, text size, tap targets and overflow, tabbed through Home with the keyboard, then read the stylesheet.
Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

What already passes: no em dashes in UI text, no fake stats or testimonials, no horizontal overflow at 390px or 1280px, a visible 3px focus ring on every control, no console errors, real empty states on Saved, and reduced-motion is respected.

## HIGH

1. **Low-contrast text in the plan builder.** R-25. Weekday labels in the date strip measure 3.17:1, the "Change" link measures 2.95:1, the unselected step tabs (Setup, Extras, Review) measure 3.91:1 and "Ex VAT · final quote follows" measures 3.44:1. All are under 4.5:1 for normal text.
2. **Low-contrast text on Home.** R-25. The "VISIONHAUS MEDIA" eyebrow measures 2.90:1 and the inactive "Rentals" or "Packages" tab measures 3.15:1.
3. **Location dropdown renders a stray "▼" under the value.** R-03 / C-4. On the plan builder the "Where's the shoot?" select shows "Windhoek" with a black triangle on a second line. Cause: the experimental `appearance: base-select` block at the end of `style.css` renders the picker icon as text in current Chromium.
4. **Header controls under 44px.** R-03. "Find a package" and "Build a custom production" are 36×36, the logo button is 40px tall and the greeting button is 32px tall. Plan step tabs are 39px tall and rental category chips are 40px tall.
5. **Text too small to read comfortably.** R-03 / C-4. 8 to 11px text appears throughout: the greeting subline is 9px, the "From" labels and eyebrow are 9px, and on phones the stylesheet drops package descriptions to 8 to 10px at 350px wide. Body copy under 12px fails the "readable on a phone" bar even where contrast passes.
6. **Floating tab bar covers content on every screen.** R-03. It sits over the "Music videos" card on Home, over the form fields in My Plan, and over the disclaimer text on the brief screen. On desktop the phone tab bar floats in the middle of a 1280px page over the form.
7. **Two date pickers for one question.** C-2 / C-4. The Scope step shows a 7-day date strip titled "Preferred shoot date" and, below it, a second "Preferred shoot date" field. A client cannot tell which one counts.

## MEDIUM

8. **Three accent colours competing.** R-29 / R-01. Periwinkle (`#8b9ade`, `#53679c`, `#e3e7f8` and more) marks selection, lime (`#e3ef8c`) marks the active tab and the main button on package detail, and sage green (`#eaf0e3`) marks the brief card. The design notes say "accent only selected controls and primary actions", so one accent should do both jobs.
9. **Icon-in-a-tinted-square on every row.** R-14 / R-04. Package cards, plan project tiles, choice rows, rental rows and the package detail header all use the same Lucide glyph inside the same pale rounded square. Six packages look like six copies of one card, and the big 70px icon tile on package detail stands in for a photo.
10. **Every icon is stock Lucide.** R-04. Most are relevant (camera, mic, scissors) but the header search and sliders icons are misleading: the "search" button does not search, it opens Home, and the sliders open the plan builder.
11. **Decorative aperture symbol behind the headline.** R-22 / R-07. A pale rotated aperture icon sits behind "Find your next production." It carries no information. The real VisionHaus mark already contains a lens, so this repeats the logo badly.
12. **Glass, gradient and inner shadows on the tab bar highlight.** R-10 / R-13. The lime pill uses a gradient, two inner shadows, a drop shadow and `backdrop-filter`, on top of a blurred dark bar. That is four effects for one "you are here" marker.
13. **Arrows on most actions.** R-08. Continue →, Choose package →, Continue to My Plan →, Build your plan →, ↗ on the brief card and a chevron on every project tile.
14. **Uppercase tracked eyebrow labels.** R-06. "VISIONHAUS MEDIA" and "YOUR PRODUCTION" at 9 to 10px with wide letter-spacing repeat what the heading already says.
15. **Highlighter mark on the headline.** R-01. The lime `<mark>` on "production." is the most common AI landing-page flourish of 2025 and 2026. It is the only use of lime outside the tab bar.

## LOW

16. **Everything is a pill.** R-11. Buttons, chips, step tabs, segment buttons, date strip, tab bar, cards (19 to 30px radius) and inputs (14px) all read as one rounded shape. There is no radius hierarchy between containers and controls.
17. **The stylesheet is four redesigns stacked.** R-31 / C-1. `style.css` redefines `:root` three times (base, V7, V12), styles `#tab-bar` 23 separate times and uses about 130 different hex colours. Later layers override earlier ones, so nobody can tell which value is live without opening dev tools.
18. **Staged and generic copy.** R-15 / R-16, plus your writing rules. "Hi, creator / Let's make your next production" (greeting for a user the app does not know), "Find your next production.", "Film, photos and everything in between.", "Equipment hire, without the guesswork.", "The moments that matter", "Shape your shoot".
19. **Brand mark forced to flat black.** R-20. The official logo gets `filter: brightness(0)` in the header and on Studio. On Studio it also becomes a 220px hero, which makes that tab feel like a splash screen.
20. **Navigation overlaps.** R-24 / C-3. Home has Packages/Rentals tabs, the bottom bar has Home/My Plan/Saved/Studio, and the header has two more icon shortcuts to Home and My Plan. "Studio" uses a person icon, which reads as an account page.
21. **Mixed VAT basis on one list.** C-4. Reels, music videos and podcasts are shown tax inclusive, the rest ex VAT, and the plan summary is ex VAT. Copy and price display, not a style issue, but it undermines a "premium" feel.

## Not touched without your say-so

- Prices, packages and the quote flow logic stay as they are.
