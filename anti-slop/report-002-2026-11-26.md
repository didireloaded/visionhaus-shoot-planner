## Design acceptance

This was a behavior-preserving refactor, not a new design. Three retained product choices are the owner's production stills on package browsing, the company profile's navy/paper/blue hierarchy, and phone-docked navigation with a desktop header alternative. No generic feature grid, decorative icon tiles or new effects were introduced. The inspected main screens retained readable primary actions and no layout hard fail was found at the two requested widths.

Not run: real iOS Safari, Android hardware, virtual keyboard, screen-reader traversal, a new installed-PWA cycle or live Zoho/Web3Forms/provider delivery. API/SQL regressions and local browser checks do not certify those environments. Email receipt remains outside this refactor and is not claimed fixed.

This round has not been published to the client-facing app. The source changes and deployable archive are ready for review on `antislop-ui-pass`.
