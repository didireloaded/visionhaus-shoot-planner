## Booking and Pricing Types

Different services should support different charging methods, rather than applying one price model to the whole catalogue.

| Offering type | Example | Suggested booking / pricing units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production service | Music video, corporate film, photography session | Per project, half-day, full day or shoot day |
| Post-production service | Editing, grading, retouching | Per video, photo, edit day, hour or project |
| Equipment rental | Camera, lens, lighting kit | Per rental day, weekend or agreed rental period |
| Crew hire | DP, gaffer, camera assistant | Half-day, full day, multiple days or project |
| Package | Event package, product campaign | Fixed bundle with optional extras |
| Retainer | Monthly social media content | Monthly recurring agreement |
| Custom quotation | Feature film, TV commercial, multi-location campaign | Quote after brief, scope and production requirements |

## Recommended Booking Fields

For each bookable item, store:

- Service or equipment name
- Category and subcategory
- Short client-facing description
- Service type: production, post-production, rental, crew, package or retainer
- Booking duration or quantity
- Base rate and pricing unit (in NAD)
- Included deliverables or equipment items
- Optional add-ons and their rates
- Overtime rate (where applicable)
- Availability, scheduling and lead time
- Crew and equipment dependencies
- Travel, transport and accommodation charges (when applicable)
- Deposit and payment terms
- Cancellation, rescheduling and rental terms
- Number of revisions (for editing/deliverables)
- Estimated delivery turnaround
- Custom quote option for complex productions
- Active / inactive status so services can be offered only when available

**Rental-specific fields:** make/model, inventory ID, quantity, condition, kit contents, daily rate, replacement value, security deposit where applicable, pickup/return dates, check-out/check-in condition, and maintenance or unavailability blocks.

**Important:** A service appearing in this master catalogue does not mean staff, facilities or equipment are currently available. Configure availability and rental inventory before enabling direct online bookings.
